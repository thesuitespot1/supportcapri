Meet Capri

A heart in the community.

Capri is a doula, community birth worker, mom, wife, and lifelong advocate for women’s health. She has poured her energy into supporting families, guiding births, uplifting mothers, and creating safe spaces for women to be heard, held, and empowered.

Her work is rooted in compassion, strength, and service—and now, it is our turn to show up for her. Even during this challenging season, Capri continues to shine with humor, softness, resilience, and the same love she has always given to others.

Her health journey

Understanding what Capri is facing.

Capri has been diagnosed with Wegener’s Granulomatosis, now commonly called granulomatosis with polyangiitis (GPA). It is a rare, serious autoimmune disease in which inflammation can affect blood vessels and vital organs, including the lungs, kidneys, and sinuses.

💉

Intensive treatment

Capri is receiving two forms of chemotherapy once a week as part of her treatment plan. This intensive care is intended to calm the immune system, protect her organs, and support long-term healing.

🩺

A serious condition

GPA can be life-threatening when inflammation damages vital organs. It requires consistent medical care, close monitoring, rest, and support throughout treatment and recovery.

♥

Care beyond appointments

Donations will help ease the practical pressures that can come with a long treatment journey, allowing Capri and her family to focus on healing.

Capri’s journey is serious, but she is not walking it alone. She is meeting this season with courage, faith, humor, and the love of her family and community.
